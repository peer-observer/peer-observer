Slow block validation on Signet: BIP-54 demo stream

A monitoring setup with two nodes: Bitcoin Inquisition (BIP-54 implemented) and Bitcoin Core (unfixed). There will be three runs at different times — during each, there will be a few slow-to-validate blocks. The Bitcoin Core takes tens of seconds to process them while Inquisition rejects them immediately. Each run ends with a reorg that removes the slow blocks from the chain.
